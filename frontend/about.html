About Our Mission

Transforming Campus Transportation

TRANSITX provides smart, punctual, and safe transit solutions engineered exclusively for college students, faculty, and campus authorities.

Our Purpose

Dedicated to Safe & Transparent Campus Mobility

College transit presents unique challenges: students waiting at distant bus stops without arrival visibility, paper bus passes being misplaced or falsified, and transport managers struggling to oversee fleet operations manually.

TRANSITX unifies these silos into a modern digital platform. With interactive OpenStreetMap tracking, simulated GPS algorithms, digital bus pass issuance, and role-based portals for students, drivers, and administrators, we make every college commute dependable.
